The supplied aura-core-weave-main.zip was inspected and not copied wholesale. Its reusable motion concepts were adapted into SAMVID’s existing Home.tsx scene: phase-based field expansion, shader-like Fresnel/grid/scan/breath treatment, translucent spherical field layers, multi-axis orbital trails, floating data panels, peripheral organization nodes, and animated consent particles.

The updated SAMVID dormant preview remains a sparse, sleek navy/graphite app shell with the existing logo, LOGIN, ENTER SAMVID, and ORGANIZATION GATEWAY controls. The active preview shows the smaller glossy central humanoid inside a large wireframe globe with a strong teal inner hologram volume, blue-teal orbit paths, eight floating record panels, five external organizations, and consent prompts. The active state was reached by touching the central identity in the browser. TypeScript, Vitest, and the production build pass.

The uploaded project’s standalone shell, routing, demo auth, and UI panels were intentionally not imported; SAMVID’s existing app architecture and owner-flow wiring remain the source of truth.
