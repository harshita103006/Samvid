# Aura Core Weave Reference Traits

Source inspected: https://aura-core-weave.lovable.app/

The live reference uses a deep midnight/navy canvas with a sparse star field and a small, bright white glossy humanoid centered inside a large spherical identity field. In dormant state, the figure stands alone with only a faint local halo and no visible records or organizations. A small upper-left status rail identifies the identity field as dormant and instructs the user to touch the identity. Entry controls sit low and centered.

In active state, the humanoid becomes smaller relative to the composition and the field expands around it. The main protective volume is a luminous translucent teal-blue inner sphere surrounded by a larger dark-blue wireframe globe. Many fine latitude/longitude-like arcs and elliptic orbital paths create a layered spherical weave. Faint star points and small square particles move through the depth of the sphere.

Eight floating data attributes are placed around the humanoid: IDENTITY, ACADEMIC, EMPLOYMENT, FINANCIAL, MEDICAL, LEGAL, RESEARCH, and CORPORATE. Each attribute is a translucent rectangular panel with a cyan/blue diamond-like core, thin luminous edges, and a small uppercase label. Fine straight and curved network lines connect records across the field, with tiny particles distributed along the paths.

External organizations remain outside the sphere. The active reference shows UNIVERSITY at upper-left, BANK at lower-left, RESEARCH ORG at upper-right, COMPANY at right, and HOSPITAL at lower-right. Each node is a geometric wireframe tower enclosing a purple-blue glowing core. Each has an uppercase organization label and the instruction CLICK TO GRANT CONSENT. The node positions are intentionally peripheral and balanced around the central field.

The active UI status reads IDENTITY FIELD / ACTIVE, 8 ATTRIBUTES INSIDE THE FIELD, and 0 CONSENT LINKS OPEN. The primary action changes to SEAL IDENTITY, while ORGANIZATION GATEWAY remains available. The reference explicitly tells the user to select a node in space or use the gateway to grant/revoke consent. The scene supports drag-to-orbit and scroll-to-zoom guidance, but SAMVID must preserve its own existing interaction model and routes.

Implementation direction: replace the current SAMVID field composition with this closer spatial arrangement, including a smaller active humanoid, larger centered field, eight record panels, five external organization towers, teal inner volume, blue wireframe globe, orbital arcs, and the reference’s bottom status/action arrangement. Keep SAMVID’s graphite/slate identity and teal/blue/lavender accents restrained, and preserve touch activation, create/revoke consent, reverse collapse, login, protected owner gates, and organization gateway behavior.
